Exhibit 10

FFA jacket, US mail bag

Vintage FFA Jacket
The FFA (Future Farmers of America) was founded in 1928. Its iconic blue corduroy jacket became the official uniform in 1933 and has since become an important symbol of American agricultural education and high school culture. Vintage FFA jackets often feature hand-embroidered designs, school names, and the wearer's name, giving them a distinctive American vintage character.

Vintage U.S. Mail Bag
Classic U.S. mail bags were used by American postal workers and were typically made from heavy-duty canvas or leather, emphasizing durability and practicality. Today, vintage mail bags are highly recognizable accessories in vintage workwear, carrying a strong sense of American workwear and postal history.
