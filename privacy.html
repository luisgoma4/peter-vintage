Privacy Policy

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller for this website is FunnyFace SL, registered in Madrid, Spain, trading online as Peter Vintage. You can reach us at funnyfacesl@pm.me for anything on this page.

2. What this site is

This site is informational and promotional only. There is no account creation, no shopping cart, no checkout and no form to fill in — the only way to buy is on Vinted, and Vinted's own privacy policy governs that purchase.

3. What we collect today

As things stand, simply browsing this site does not collect or store any personal data through cookies or browser storage — see the Cookie Policy for the technical detail.

If you choose to email us at funnyfacesl@pm.me, we process the contents of your message and your email address only to reply to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries; we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to handle it, then delete it.

4. Planned analytics

We plan to add Umami, a cookieless, privacy-first analytics tool, to see aggregate figures such as page views, referrers and device type, and how many visitors click through to Vinted. Umami does not use cookies or persistent tracking identifiers and does not build a profile of any individual visitor. As a precaution, and independently of Umami's cookieless design, this site shows an on-screen notice about it — see the on-site banner and the Cookie Policy.

5. Third-party platforms

Buying a piece happens entirely on Vinted; following the shop happens on Instagram. Both are independent platforms with their own privacy policies, outside our control — please review theirs before using them.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and the right to data portability, for any data we hold about you (in practice, this means correspondence you have sent us). To exercise any of these rights, write to funnyfacesl@pm.me.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), or your own country's equivalent authority.

7. International transfers

We do not currently transfer any personal data outside the European Economic Area in connection with this site.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if what this site collects changes — for instance, once Umami analytics goes live. Check the date below for the latest revision.
